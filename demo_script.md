# EventSaathi AI: Video Demonstration Script
> **Challenge Round:** PromptWars Challenge 5 (GenAI Celebration & Event Planner)  
> **Target Video Duration:** 2.5 - 3 minutes  
> **Scenario:** 30th Birthday Celebration in Ahmedabad, Gujarat (50 Guests, ₹50,000 INR)

---

### Segment 1: Introduction (0:00 - 0:25)
- **Visual:** Open browser at `http://127.0.0.1:5173/`. Landing hero displays the warm celebratory maroon/rose visual design.
- **Narration:**
  > *"Hello judges! Welcome to **EventSaathi AI**, our GenAI-powered celebration and event planner built for weddings, birthdays, and community festivals. Unlike standard chatbots that just output plain text lists, EventSaathi provides an editable, persistent planning master plan with decimal-safe budget intelligence, interactive milestone timelines, and vendor coordination workspaces—fully localized in English, Hindi, and Gujarati."*

---

### Segment 2: Event Creation & AI Plan Generation (0:25 - 0:55)
- **Visual:** Click **"Load Sample Celebration (Ahmedabad Birthday)"** or click **"+ Plan Event"** to show the 3-step wizard.
  - Step 1: Event Name: *"Aarav's 30th Birthday Celebration"*, City: *"Ahmedabad, Gujarat"*, Guests: *50*.
  - Step 2: Budget: *₹50,000 INR*, Language: *English*.
  - Step 3: Theme: *"Bollywood Retro & Festive Elegance"*, Dietary: *"Pure Vegetarian with Jain counter"*.
  - Click **"Generate Celebration Master Plan ✨"**. Confetti bursts and the Dashboard renders with real-time stats.
- **Narration:**
  > *"With a single click, our server-side engine synthesizes an entire structured event plan. Notice the status badge indicating our active planning engine. If an API key is present, Google Gemini generates the creative themes; if offline or during network disruptions, our intelligent deterministic fallback planner activates seamlessly with zero crashes."*

### Segment 4: Decimal-Safe Budget Intelligence (1:25 - 1:55)
- **Visual:**
  - Click **"Budget Planner"** tab.
  - Show the 4 summary cards: Total Budget (₹50,000), Estimated Total (₹50,000), Actual Spent, and Remaining Balance.
  - Point out the percentage allocation breakdown across Venue, Catering, Decor, Photography, Entertainment, and Reserves.
  - Click **"+ Add Expense Item"** or edit an existing item (change actual spent to ₹12,500 and check "Paid").
  - Click **"Export to CSV"**. Open the downloaded CSV to show the sanitized spreadsheet report with formula injection defense.
- **Narration:**
  > *"Next is our Budget Planner. Notice that the sum of all category allocations equals exactly ₹50,000. All calculations use decimal-safe arithmetic computed by backend application code. We can track actual spent amounts, toggle payment statuses, and immediately identify if we're over budget. We can also export our budget to CSV, protected with automated formula injection sanitization."*

### Segment 5: Timeline, Tasks & Vendor Hub (1:55 - 2:30)
- **Visual:**
  - Click **"Timeline & Tasks"** tab.
  - Click the checkbox next to the first task (*"Confirm & book venue in Ahmedabad"*).
  - Watch the progress bar instantly animate from 0% to 16.7%.

- **Narration:**
  > *"Under Timeline & Tasks, milestones are organized by priority and preparation phases. When we check off a task, progress recalculates dynamically. In the Vendor Hub, organizers compare quotations against criteria—such as verifying food tastings and sound permits—without fabricated fake listings."*

### Segment 6: Persistence & Fallback Demonstration (2:30 - 2:55)
- **Visual:**
  - Press browser **Refresh (F5)**.
  - The page reloads: Aarav's 30th Birthday Celebration is still active, the completed task remains checked, and the updated budget expense is completely preserved.
  - Click **Settings** in the top right to show the AI Engine Status modal confirming that the deterministic fallback engine is 100% operational.
- **Narration:**
  > *"When we refresh the page, all progress, custom vendors, and expense entries persist securely in SQLite. EventSaathi AI provides a truly reliable, complete celebration planning assistant that never fails when you need it most. Thank you!"*
